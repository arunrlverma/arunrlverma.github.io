The plan, in two moves

An approved drug and a personal vaccine

This is my treatment story, not proof that the vaccine, or the combination, improves outcomes.

01

Vorasidenib

My tumor has IDH1 R132C. Vorasidenib is FDA-approved for grade 2 astrocytoma or oligodendroglioma with a susceptible IDH1 or IDH2 mutation after surgery. It is the IDH inhibitor I am on now.

FDA indication INDIGO trial
02

Personal neoantigen vaccine

The vaccine is built from my tumor's own mutations, so it only exists for me. It's made of peptides that cross the mutated spots, the idea being to point my T cells at fragments of protein that shouldn't be in a healthy body. Nobody knows yet whether it helps, or whether pairing it with the IDH drug helps.

Personal benefit unknown NCI overview

Evidence reviewed July 13, 2026.

Why this feels different to me: two mechanisms at once. Quiet the mutant IDH enzyme and drop its 2-HG output, while separately teaching the immune system to spot a handful of tumor-specific peptides. The vaccine half, and running both together, is still unproven.
